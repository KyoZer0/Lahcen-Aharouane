# Portfolio favicon

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Master: public/generated/favicon-la.png. App Router assets: app/favicon.ico, app/icon.png, app/apple-icon.png.

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: a production favicon for Lahcen Aharouane's personal developer portfolio.
Generate one finished square icon, not a presentation sheet or mockup.
Subject: a strong custom geometric monogram reading exactly "LA" (L then A), compact and tightly interlocked, instantly legible at 16 and 32 pixels. White heavyweight angular letters with broad strokes and generous open counters on a near-black #101010 square tile. One decisive hot-pink #ff006c diagonal print-cut stripe crosses the lower third of the letters, introducing a small controlled offset like a torn street poster.
Art direction: minimal Swiss typography with Watch Dogs 2 inspired hacker-poster energy. Original personal LA branding only. Sharp flat shapes; strong silhouette. The icon's dark tile occupies almost the entire canvas, with small diagonal clipped corners and actual transparent pixels outside those corners. The white monogram occupies about 76% of the tile width and 65% height, optically centered; generous internal margins.
Colors: near black, off-white #f4f4f3, hot pink #ff006c only.
Constraints: exactly one icon centered in a square image; flat front view; crisp edges; no photographic textures, halftone dots, tiny details, gradients, shadows, 3D, badges, surrounding objects, additional words, watermarks, or game logos. Do not include a border or tiny dot after the initials. Prioritize a clean silhouette that remains recognizable at tiny favicon size.
